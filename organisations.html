For organisations

Maps, analysis and tools for people who look after assets.

Catchments, terrain, river levels and asset registers, turned into maps you can read, charts that answer a question, and tools your team can use. Below is the kind of thing I build, not a description of it.

What you are looking at. None of these figures is client work. Three are built from open Environment Agency data for upper Wharfedale in the Yorkshire Dales and are marked Open data. Four are concepts and are marked Concept: one replays the same open readings through an interface, and three use a made-up stock of structures. Every figure is drawn in code, so it can be rebuilt when the data changes.

How it is made

Open data, plain code, nothing you cannot check.

Each figure on this page comes out of a short script. Run it again with new data and the figure updates.

TerrainEnvironment Agency LIDAR Composite DTM at 1 m, fetched for the area needed. The catchment map uses it resampled to 20 m.
River and rainEnvironment Agency hydrology service: 15-minute level at Kettlewell, Starbotton and Oughtershaw Beck, and rainfall at Beckermonds. Readings are shown as published, before the Agency's quality checks.
Catchment and streamsWorked out from the terrain with WhiteboxTools: drainage direction, flow accumulation, then the area upstream of the gauge.
Asset figuresA made-up stock from a seeded generator, so the same figures come out every time. It describes no real asset, place or organisation.
DrawingPython writing SVG directly. No screenshots and no image files, so every figure stays sharp at any size.
Your dataStays yours. I work on a copy you control, and the scripts and outputs are handed over with the work.
