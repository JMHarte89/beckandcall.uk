How it is made

Open data, plain code, nothing you cannot check.

Each figure on this page comes out of a short script. Run it again with new data and the figure updates.

Dated LIDAREnvironment Agency time-stamped 1 m terrain tiles, flown 15 September 2008 and 20 April 2023. One subtracted from the other gives the change.
Point cloudsThe Environment Agency's LAZ point cloud tiles for the same two flights, read directly and drawn point by point.
SeabedThe Environment Agency SurfZone DEM 2019, a published 2 m surface that joins land LIDAR to seabed data. It is a ready-made product, not a survey I processed from soundings.
Terrain analysisEnvironment Agency LIDAR Composite, with drainage direction and flow accumulation worked out in WhiteboxTools.
River and rainEnvironment Agency hydrology service, 15-minute readings, shown as published before the Agency's quality checks.
RegisterBridge and culvert locations, road names and watercourse names from OpenStreetMap. Condition grades, dates and ratings come from a seeded generator and are not real.
DrawingPython writing SVG directly. No screenshots and no image files, so every figure stays sharp at any size.
Your dataStays yours. I work on a copy you control, and the scripts and outputs are handed over with the work.
